About Sentinel

Better business should mean better business for everyone

Sentinel Business Solutions was founded on a simple belief: that the way a business succeeds matters as much as the success itself.

Organisations can be profitable, ambitious and competitive without compromising their principles. Growth should not come at the expense of people, communities or the planet. Compliance should not be a box-ticking exercise, and good governance should mean more than meeting the minimum the law requires.

Our purpose is to help organisations become better, not only in how they operate but in the effect they have on everything around them. We do that through consultancy, management systems, document packs, readiness tools and published guidance.

How we work

Advice that does not create work for us

Most compliance advice has a conflict built into it. The consultant who tells you what you need is usually the same one who sells it to you, and the longer the list, the better their year.

We would rather tell you what you do not need.

The assessments are free

No account, no email address, no card. They will tell you where you already meet a standard as readily as where you do not. If a gap can be closed by rewording a policy you already have, we would rather say so than sell you a document.

The charity standard is given away

ECOS runs to fourteen domains, 117 requirements and twenty-two templates, written for the charity sector and free in full. No payment, no certification body charging thousands for a certificate. If it helps, we ask you to give £25 to a charity instead. Nothing checks whether you did.

Nothing is locked

Every document is an ordinary Word or Excel file. Nothing is watermarked, nothing expires and nothing needs our software to open. Rename it, rebrand it, cut it about. Once you have bought it, it is yours.

Integrity at the centre

Doing the right thing when nobody is watching

Businesses should be honest in their decisions, transparent in their actions and accountable for their impact. That means doing the right thing when it is inconvenient, when it is difficult, and when nobody is looking.

Standards like ISO give organisations a framework for that, but the framework is not the point. What matters is what it represents: consistency, responsibility, evidence, improvement and trust. A certificate on the wall only really has meaning if the organisation lives up to what it stands for.

People before processes

Organisations succeed because of people

Policies and procedures matter, but they are not what makes an organisation work.

Employees should be treated with dignity, respect and fairness. Workplaces should protect physical and psychological wellbeing. People should be able to contribute ideas, raise concerns and take part in improving the place they work.

The strongest organisations are not the ones that demand most from their people. They are the ones that build conditions in which people can do their best work.

Who we are
